# Hackathon

Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon (Meta & Cerebral Valley). Source: the event page and the
Official Rules / Event Waiver at https://cerebralvalley.ai/e/meta-ai-developer-hackathon/waiver.

## Our entry

- **Track:** Indie Developer (individuals, or teams of up to 4). Solo entry.
- **Theme:** Build for Your World: "solve a problem you know firsthand, free up time and mental space, and make something possible that wasn't before."
- **Problem:** long solo workdays alone with a screen are draining, and the pet who keeps you sane isn't there. Existing desktop pets are stock characters that can't see what you're doing. PixyPup turns one photo of your own pet into a companion that lives on your Mac and keeps an eye on your work with you (sniffs failing tests, perks at messages, hides on calls).

## Judging (official)

| Criterion | Weight | What they look for | Where PixyPup shows it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Impact | 30% | Real-world potential, who benefits, could people use it, fits a problem statement | The firsthand hook; one-photo setup anyone can do; privacy-first (screen and voice off by default) |
| Use of Meta AI tooling | 25% | Creative use, beyond a basic integration, surfacing unexpected capabilities | Five models chained: SAM 3.1 cutout, Muse Image poses and walk cycles, Muse Wonder reviewing every pose (redrawing misfits) and judging which walk looks most natural, Muse Spark reactions and fix hints, Voice Transcribe |
| Technical depth & execution | 20% | Sound, refined engineering, real craft | Click-through overlay, native helper, stride-matched walk, offline fallbacks, 675 tests |
| Demo | 25% | Working and impressive, holds up live, video quality | The failing-test sniff, live; the debug card proves it is real |

"Holds up live" matters for the Nov 9 finals: keep the recording machine's setup working through then.
